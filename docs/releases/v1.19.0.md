Search that finds the game you mean, a search keyboard for controllers, and clearer
notifications.

## New
- **Search forgives how you type.** Words together or apart (`spiderman`, `witcher3`),
  numbers either way (`final fantasy 7` finds VII), initials and nicknames (`tlou`, `re4`,
  `gow`, `cod`, `spidey`), and genres (`racing`, `horror`). A typo still finds the game:
  `assasins creed` shows Assassin's Creed and says what it searched for.
- **Best matches first.** A game whose name starts with what you typed comes before one that
  only contains it; your chosen sort orders the games that match equally well.
- **Search with a controller.** Press △ and an on-screen keyboard comes up under the results,
  which update with every letter. The row above the keys has the best matches to open
  straight away, or your recent searches before you type. □ deletes, △ adds a space, R2 shows
  the results. Searching from a keyboard works as before.

## Improved
- **Notifications** say who they're from, glide in and out, show the time they have left, and
  a button says what clicking does.
- **Update notifications take you to the update.** "Version X is ready" opens Settings on the
  Azrak Launcher row, where one press restarts into the new version. "Update available" goes
  to the same row.

## Fixed
- A double-click on a notification no longer clicks the screen it opens (it could switch on
  **Download only through a VPN**).
- Clicking a notification no longer clicks what's behind it.

## Update
Launchers on v1.3.0 or later update themselves if automatic updates are enabled. Otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher/releases/latest/download/azrak-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./azrak-linux-x86_64/install.sh
```
