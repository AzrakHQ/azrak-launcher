A big update: trailers play inside the launcher, the launcher tells you how games will be
controlled, downloads keep seeding after a restart, and crashed games' logs go to KytyPS5.

## Added
- **Trailers inside the launcher:** official trailers now play fullscreen in the launcher itself,
	with a progress bar. **✕** (Enter) pauses, **◂ ▸** skip 10 seconds, **○** (Esc) closes, and
	clicking the bar jumps to that point. Trailers need mpv (`sudo apt install mpv`); without it
	the Trailer button is hidden instead of opening your browser.
- **Controllers:** while a game starts, the launcher shows which controller it will get, or, with
	none connected, the keyboard keys games use. **Settings → Keyboard controls in games** shows the
	full layout. Games play best with a controller: DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox and most others work
	over USB or Bluetooth with no setup.
- **Crash logs for KytyPS5:** when a game crashes or closes right after starting, its log is kept
	instead of being overwritten by the next launch. It goes with your rating when you choose
	**Settings → Share your results**, and the reports lead with KytyPS5's error message.
- **Clickable notifications:** click a crash notification to see its log, a download notification
	to open Downloads, or the "You've rated games" reminder to share your results.

## Improved
- **Seeding keeps going:** completed downloads seed again whenever the launcher opens, as long as
	**Settings → Seed completed downloads** is on. Turning that setting off is the only way to stop
	seeding for good; turning it back on resumes all of them. Before seeding, the launcher checks the
	files, and a download whose files were moved or changed is never downloaded again.
- **Sharing results:** the logs to attach are gathered in one folder, named after each game.

## Update
Launchers on v1.3.0 or later update themselves if automatic updates are enabled. Otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher/releases/latest/download/azrak-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./azrak-linux-x86_64/install.sh
```
