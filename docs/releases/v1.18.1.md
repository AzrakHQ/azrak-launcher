The battery, for laptops.

## New
- **Battery level next to the clock.** On a laptop the top bar shows how much battery is
  left: an upright battery with the percentage, a bolt while it charges, amber at 20% or
  less and red at 10% or less. PCs without a battery show nothing.
- **A notice when it runs low:** at 20%, and again at 10% ("save your game"), while it's not
  plugged in.

## Improved
- The search button on the Home screen is gone: it only led to the Library, which has its
  own search. △ (or / on a keyboard) still jumps straight to it.

## Update
Launchers on v1.3.0 or later update themselves if automatic updates are enabled. Otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher/releases/latest/download/azrak-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./azrak-linux-x86_64/install.sh
```
