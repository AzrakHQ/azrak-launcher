PS5 Launcher is now **Azrak Launcher**, with a new logo. Your games, downloads, saves and
settings come with you.

## New name, same launcher
- **Azrak Launcher** (أزرق, "blue") has a new name and logo, and a new home:
  [github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher](https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher). The PC edition
  is now **AzrakOS** ([github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-os](https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-os)).
- **Everything moves over by itself** the first time Azrak Launcher starts:
  - your settings, emulators, saves, Wine prefixes and caches move to `~/.config/azrak`,
    `~/.local/share/azrak` and `~/.cache/azrak`;
  - your game and download folders, if you kept the defaults, become `~/Games/Azrak` and
    `~/Downloads/Azrak` (folders you chose yourself stay where they are);
  - downloads in progress, installed games and play time follow them;
  - the app menu entry and icon become Azrak Launcher, and the command is now `azrak`.

  Nothing is left behind under the old names.

## Improved
- **PS4 exclusives in the Library:** God of War III Remastered and 140 more PS4 exclusives
  that were missing from the game list are in (they arrived with the game list's update, no
  launcher update needed).

## Update
Launchers on v1.3.0 or later update themselves if automatic updates are enabled. Otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher/releases/latest/download/azrak-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./azrak-linux-x86_64/install.sh
```
