## Fixed
- **Covers from the forum load again.** Most releases' own covers are on fastpic.org, which
  stopped answering the launcher's requests. About 4,600 releases showed a plain placeholder
  instead (eFootball PES 2021 among them). They show their cover again.

## Improved
- **More PC games get their details and artwork.** Steam's search leaves out games that are no
  longer sold (eFootball PES 2021, Deadpool, WWE 2K15) and some with short names. These are now
  found another way, so roughly 300 more PC games get their About, screenshots and key art.
  They arrive with the catalog over the next days; there's no need to update for this part.

## Update
Launchers on v1.3.0 or later update themselves if automatic updates are enabled. Otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher/releases/latest/download/azrak-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./azrak-linux-x86_64/install.sh
```
