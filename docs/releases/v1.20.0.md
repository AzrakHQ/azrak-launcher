## New
- **One card per game.** A game released for several platforms is one card in the Library,
  with a badge for each platform (PS5, PS4, PC). The Library is about a thousand cards shorter.
- **Play it on.** A game's page has a tile for each platform: how it should play on this PC,
  how easy it is to download and its size. **Best for this PC** marks the one to pick, and
  choosing a tile shows the game there. Each version keeps its own saves.
- **The right version first.** A card opens on the version you have installed, else on the one
  that should play best here and can be downloaded. On a tie: PC (no emulator), then PS4, then
  PS5.

## How it decides
- PS5 and PS4 versions: the emulator communities' results and what this PC has.
- PC versions: native Linux games first, then Wine with ProtonDB's rating.
- Nothing more is promised than that: no guessed frame rates.
- Same-name games years apart stay separate: Prey (2006) and Prey (2017), the 1996 Tomb Raider
  and the 2013 one, the classic Doom and Doom (2016).

## Improved
- A release without its own pictures shows the game's official art from another release.
- Director's Cut and "+ DLC" or soundtrack bundles count as editions of the game.

## Update
Launchers on v1.3.0 or later update themselves if automatic updates are enabled. Otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher/releases/latest/download/azrak-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./azrak-linux-x86_64/install.sh
```
