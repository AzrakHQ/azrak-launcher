Rate how your games run, share your results with the KytyPS5 community, and find every playable
game in one click.

## Added
- **Your own results:** when you close a game, the launcher asks how far it got. You can choose
	**Not now**, and it won't ask again on the same KytyPS5 build. Rate any game anytime from
	**Options → Rate how it runs**. Your answer becomes the game's tag on your PC right away, for
	example **In-game on Linux · your result**, and the Game Hub shows it next to the community's
	results.
- **Share your results:** **Settings → Share your results with KytyPS5** opens a pre-filled
	KytyPS5 report for each game you rated: the result, the KytyPS5 build, your PC's details and
	the end of the emulator log. Check each one, attach the log and submit. If results are waiting,
	the launcher reminds you about once a week.
- **In-game filter:** the Library's new **In-game** filter shows every game that reaches gameplay
	on any system. **In-game on Linux** still shows only games confirmed on Linux.

## Fixed
- **Menu icon:** launchers that updated themselves kept the icon of the version first installed.
	The launcher now puts the current icon in place when it starts. If your dock still shows the
	old one, log out and back in.

## Notes
- Sharing needs a free GitHub account, and each game is its own report. Nothing is sent until you
	submit it.
- Your results are saved in `~/.config/azrak/results.json`.
- The README is shorter and written for players. It now shows the `azrak` command.

## Update
Launchers on v1.3.0 or later update themselves if automatic updates are enabled. Otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/AzrakHQ/azrak-launcher/releases/latest/download/azrak-linux-x86_64.tar.gz | tar xz && ./azrak-linux-x86_64/install.sh
```
